## Lab requirements

The following requirements describe the necessary deliverables for the Event Logs Lab.

| Criteria                                                                                                                  | Points |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| The operating system of your device is listed in the <code class="filepath">README.md</code> file.                        | 10     |
| A screen capture of your operating system's event viewer is included in the <code class="filepath">README.md</code> file. | 50     |
| One of the reflection prompts is answered in the <code class="filepath">README.md</code> file.                            | 40     |
| **Total possible points**                                                                                                 | 100    |
